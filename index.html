About JB TV

JB TV is a free live-TV web player. Browse thousands of publicly available IPTV channels from around the world, search by name, filter by category or language, and watch in a fast, clean player.

  • Live previews on hover or press-and-hold
  • Draggable mini-player, Picture-in-Picture and fullscreen
  • Multiple audio tracks, brightness and aspect-ratio controls
  • Light and dark themes, responsive on every screen
  • Installable as an app (Install button, or Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone)

Shortcuts: Space/K play·pause, F fullscreen, M mute, A aspect, / search, Esc back.

JB TV does not host, store or upload any video. Channels come from the public iptv-org playlists and belong to their respective owners; some may be offline or region-locked. Player powered by HLS.js.
